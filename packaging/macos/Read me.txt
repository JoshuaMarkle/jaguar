JAGUAR for Mac
==============

Finds the lightest concrete canoe wall (thickness, mix density, mold depth) that passes
every check under the crew. Runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs (macOS 11 or later).

START
  1. Drag JAGUAR into your Applications folder.
  2. Double-click JAGUAR. It opens in your web browser.
     The first time, macOS blocks it because it is not notarized by Apple:
       macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later: click "Done", open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
         scroll down to "JAGUAR was blocked", click "Open Anyway" and confirm.
       macOS 14 or earlier: Control-click JAGUAR, choose "Open", then "Open".
     If macOS asks whether JAGUAR may access your Documents folder, click "Allow"
     (that is where it keeps your canoe files).

USE
  * Edit the inputs on the left, press "Run optimization", read the result on the right.
    Hover any label for help.
  * It starts with the team's settings: the hull shape is searched too (thousands of hull
    shapes, each with a full wall search), so a run can take hours. For a quick look, lower
    "Samples" under the hull shape search (e.g. 96: a few minutes).
  * While a run is going, "Pause" holds it (Resume carries on) and "End" finishes it early:
    the best hull found so far is checked and shown as the result.
  * Your files are in Documents/JAGUAR: canoe.toml (the settings), the hull, the lab data
    (mix_tests.csv) and result.json (the last run's answer). The "Open folder" button at the top opens it.
    Put other hulls (jaguar-hull JSON) there to pick them in the form.
  * Settings are saved as you type.

STOP
  Press "Quit" at the top right. JAGUAR has no window or Dock icon of its own; if you just
  close the browser tab, it stops by itself after about 5 minutes. Double-click JAGUAR again
  to come back.

UPDATE
  Download the new zip and replace JAGUAR in Applications. Your Documents/JAGUAR folder is kept.

TERMINAL (optional)
  /Applications/JAGUAR.app/Contents/MacOS/jaguar run canoe.toml
  .../jaguar --help lists every command.

Source and documentation: https://github.com/JoshuaMarkle/jaguar
