JAGUAR for Windows
==================

Finds the lightest concrete canoe wall (thickness, mix density, mold depth) that passes
every check under the crew.

START
  1. Right-click the zip, choose "Extract All...", and open the extracted folder.
  2. Double-click JAGUAR.exe. JAGUAR opens in your web browser.
     The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" (the program is not
     signed by a paid certificate). Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

USE
  * Edit the inputs on the left, press "Run optimization", read the result on the right.
    Hover any label for help.
  * It starts with the team's settings: the hull shape is searched too (thousands of hull
    shapes, each with a full wall search), so a run can take hours. For a quick look, lower
    "Samples" under the hull shape search (e.g. 96: a few minutes).
  * While a run is going, "Pause" holds it (Resume carries on) and "End" finishes it early:
    the best hull found so far is checked and shown as the result.
  * Your files are in Documents\JAGUAR: canoe.toml (the settings), the hull, the lab data
    (mix_tests.csv) and result.json (the last run's answer). The "Open folder" button at the top opens it.
    Put other hulls (jaguar-hull JSON) there to pick them in the form.
  * Settings are saved as you type.

STOP
  Press "Quit" at the top right. If you just close the browser tab, JAGUAR stops by
  itself after about 5 minutes. Double-click JAGUAR.exe again to come back.

UPDATE
  Download the new zip and replace JAGUAR.exe. Your Documents\JAGUAR folder is kept.

TERMINAL (optional)
  JAGUAR.exe also works from a terminal, e.g.  JAGUAR.exe run canoe.toml
  JAGUAR.exe --help lists every command.

Source and documentation: https://github.com/JoshuaMarkle/jaguar
